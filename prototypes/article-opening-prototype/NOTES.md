Question: which B-family color and rule treatment makes Edition 4 inviting
without obscuring its provenance?

All variants use Edition 4's real MCP article, institutional byline, primary
URL, and opening paragraph. The affiliation line is taken from the
captured source copyright. It is not a newly invented biography. The variants
share the approved manga-panel geometry. They disagree only about the metadata
treatment, intro rule, and restrained spot-color system.

- A, Quiet ink: hairline metadata rules and a slim black intro rule.
- B, Cobalt and coral: a compact cobalt metadata cap and a coral intro rule.
- C, Paper and spots: unprinted white paper with no page-wide grain, one quiet
  gray separator, plus a signal-orange image shadow and metadata tick.

All three remove the visible QR label and the bottom `Feature 06` folio. The
kicker retains the feature identity. A and B also retain the large article
number; C removes it.

The illustration is the muted watercolor MCP opener sample, with exactly one
boy and one helper robot. No background color is made into an edition-wide
rule. Watercolor variation, paper tooth, quiet print grain, and a softened
cobalt, coral, ochre, green, and lavender palette make the prototype feel less
bright and more print-like.

The earlier `mcp-opener-boy-robot-color-v1.png` remains only as an iteration
record. It is not referenced because extra robot-like faces weakened cast
continuity. The bright v2 asset remains an approved character reference, but
the prototype now uses the independently approved muted watercolor asset.
