# Model card art

Below the line is a trading card for an AI model. It needs a painting: a
beautiful, majestic giant that is this model. Write four image prompts for it
and go wild.

Be surreal, playful, strange, dreamlike, funny, cosmic, tender, whatever the
idea wants. Invent: impossible scale, odd materials, weird jobs, little
visual jokes about what the card says the model does, worlds that could not
exist. Every one a different idea, a different look, a different composition:
the giant facing us, from behind, upside down, in close-up, tiny in a vast
frame, cut by the edge, mid-leap, asleep. Mix painting styles freely, with a
lean toward 1970s airbrushed science-fiction and prog-rock covers. Call the
giant a giant, colossus, or titan somewhere in each prompt.

Only limits: the giant is beautiful and has a good face and a body that looks
right; no birds; no ethnic or non-Western-culture giants; no text or
lettering in the picture; never the lab's or the model's name. At most 120
words each. Never use the em dash character (U+2014).

## Output

One JSON object, `{"art_briefs": ["...", "...", "...", "..."]}`, nothing
else, no code fence.
