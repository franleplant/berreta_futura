# Model card art

Below the line is a trading card for an AI model. It needs a painting: a
beautiful, majestic giant that is this model. Go wild.

First, under `obvious`, write down the six ideas that come to mind first for
this card, one line each. Those are what every other card would get. Then
write four image prompts under `art_briefs` that are none of them, and none
of the ideas listed as already tried below the card, if there are any.

The card is a starting point, not a script: the painting does not have to
illustrate what the model does. Surreal, strange, dreamlike, cosmic, tender,
funny: whatever the idea wants. Each of the four is its own idea, its own
world, its own composition, and its own painting style, with a lean toward
1970s airbrushed science-fiction and prog-rock covers. Call the giant a
giant, colossus, or titan somewhere in each prompt.

Only limits: the giant is beautiful and has a good face and a body that looks
right; no birds; no ethnic or non-Western-culture giants; no text or
lettering in the picture; never the lab's or the model's name. At most 120
words each. Never use the em dash character (U+2014).

## Output

One JSON object, `{"obvious": ["...", ...], "art_briefs": ["...", "...",
"...", "..."]}`, nothing else, no code fence.
