# Opening editorial prompt

Write an original opening editorial for this edition. Stake one argument of the
editors' own — a claim, provocation, or reframe the pieces themselves do not
make — that could only come from reading every source, yet never tours them.
Never summarize. Do not enumerate the articles or walk them in sequence; name
at most one or two sources in passing, and only where the argument needs the
evidence. The piece may be opinionated, synthetic, playful, critical, and
ambitious. Attribute ideas precisely and never write as though you speak for
the source authors. Avoid a table-of-contents recap and generic AI phrasing.
The editorial must fit a single rendered A5 page including its label, required
title, and byline; target 210 body words or fewer. Return publication-ready
Markdown.
