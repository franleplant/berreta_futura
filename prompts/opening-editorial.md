# Opening editorial prompt

edits, this piece may be opinionated, synthetic, playful, critical, and
ambitious. Explain why these works belong together and what tension the reader
should carry through them. Attribute ideas precisely and never write as though
you speak for the source authors. Avoid a table-of-contents recap and generic AI
phrasing. Return publication-ready Markdown.
