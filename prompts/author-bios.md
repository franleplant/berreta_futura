# Author bios

Each line below is an article: id | author | site | url. Write the line the
opener prints under the byline: who the author is, in at most 80 characters,
ending with a period. Give their role and where they work or write, for
example "Works at Anthropic.", "CEO of Replit.", "Writes the Contraptions
newsletter.", "Researchers at Anthropic." Describe several authors together.
When the author is an organization, say what it is. When the author is a
historical figure, give what they were known for and their dates.

- Never name the author: the byline above already does.
- Never describe the article or its subject.
- State only what the site and url show or what is widely documented about
  the person. When unsure of a role, say only where they publish: "Writes for
  the LangChain blog."

## Output

One line per article, in the order given, nothing else:

id | bio

Never use the em dash character (U+2014).
