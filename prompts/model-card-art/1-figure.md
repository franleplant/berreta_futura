Below are the card for an AI model and the launch post it was written from.
Write four subjects for the card's painting, each one paragraph of at most
90 words. They must read as four different paintings, not four takes on one
picture. Each opens with its shot, in this order, and follows it:

"Close portrait:" the figure from the chest up, off-centre and filling the
frame, face lit from one side, holding one thing from the post up to the
viewer; the big shape is cropped by the frame edge behind it.

"Wide vista:" the figure small, about a quarter of the frame tall, in a vast
landscape or giant structure the post suggests; the big shape owns the sky.

"Workshop interior:" indoors, no sky, the figure busy at a bench or machine
among three or four things from the post, lit by one lamp; the big shape
shows up as a round window, a dial, or a projection on the wall.

"Low angle:" seen from below, the figure striding, reaching, or launching
something on a strong diagonal, the big shape behind its head.

The figure is one person with a face, two arms and two legs. Change who it
is each time: one human in work clothes or a spacesuit, one robot or
android, one pilot or diver in a suit, and at most one made of a strange
material. No statues, no robes, no meditating, and not every figure calm.

The things around it are what the post shows the model making or doing,
drawn as physical objects, different in each painting.

The big shape is one huge simple form that reads from across a room: a sun
disk, a ringed planet, a moon, an orbit ring, a halo of stars, a comet. Use
a different one in each painting.

Each painting names its light (a time of day or a light source) and a
three-colour palette; no two palettes share a main colour.
