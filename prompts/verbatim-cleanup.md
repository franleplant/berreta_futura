# Verbatim cleanup

The article below was transcribed word for word from a web page and will be
printed in a magazine exactly as written, under its own title, byline, and
date. It is split into numbered blocks. List the blocks that are not part of
the article itself and must not be printed:

- a second copy of the intro, or a lede or deck that repeats another block
- a leftover byline, date, reading-time, or "updated" line
- calls to subscribe, follow, share, sign up, join a Discord or community,
  or watch for updates
- "read more", "related", "learn more", or "see the paper below" pointers
  that point at things the printed page cannot show
- compliance, availability, or API-endpoint boilerplate that a docs site
  appends to every page (never the article's own news about plans, prices,
  or rollout)
- site navigation, feedback prompts ("Was this page helpful?"), and footers

Keep everything the author wrote as part of the piece: the argument,
examples, lists, tables, code, closing paragraphs that conclude the piece,
footnotes, and acknowledgements. When a block mixes article text with a
pointer, keep it. When unsure, keep it.

## Output

One line per block to cut, nothing else:

N | reason

Reply `none` when every block belongs to the article.

Never use the em dash character (U+2014).
