# Article labels: short title and display emphasis

Each line below is an article id and its full title. For each, choose:

- `short_title`: the running-head label, at most 40 characters, copied
  exactly from the title (a contiguous run of its words, same spelling and
  case). Prefer the name of the thing over the scaffolding around it: drop
  "Introducing", "How", "Getting started with", subtitles after a colon,
  and parenthetical years when the rest still names the piece.
- `display_emphasis`: the one to three words of the title the opener sets
  large, copied exactly from the title: the name, the coinage, or the
  noun the piece is about. Never a function word.

## Output

One line per article, in the order given, nothing else:

id | short_title | display_emphasis

Never use the em dash character (U+2014).
