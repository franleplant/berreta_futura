`WP-5.1f.verify.md`.

## What was confirmed

- The fix is correct and complete: a null `label:` now behaves as an absent
  key in all four YAML spellings, matching `html_edition.py:737`'s
  `value is not None` guard.
- The demonstration block reproduces exactly as captioned, naming all four
  null spellings under the pre-fix body (4 passed, 3 failed), then 7 passed.
- All five guards fire under perturbation. Nine fixture edits were made and
  all nine went red, including a fixture edited into harmlessness and a new
  case added without a `status` or with an empty `why`.
- The interaction, checked by construction rather than by argument.
  WP-5.1f's padded `ARTICLE` case is survived by an implementation that
  discards the declared label and returns the fallback, so it genuinely could
  not discriminate. WP-5.1g's `Dispatch` case kills that implementation and
  two others, and is the only case in either fixture that kills all three.
  Both halves of WP-5.1g's account hold.
- The rule 10c sweep: the fallback limb has exactly one instance in these two
  fixture sets, and it is the one WP-5.1g found. The input-itself limb has
  four unflagged instances, each individually non-discriminating and each
  rescued by other cases in the set; recorded as a finding, not a hole.
- The fixture is not generated and cannot be regenerated by anything in the
  tree. Twelve of thirteen values are derivable from stated product rules;
  the thirteenth is transcribed from output and is the one flagged
  `agreed_but_suspect`.
- Corpus numbers re-derived from their enumerations: 548 `label:` keys, 0 null
  spellings, 0 booleans.
- Gate at `3b9cbcb`: 207 tests across 22 binaries, `cargo test` exit 0,
  `fmt` and `clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings` clean.
