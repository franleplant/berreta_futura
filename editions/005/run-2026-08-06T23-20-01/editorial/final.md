title: Build the Doors
byline: The Editors
---

We blamed the mind. It was the room.

A worker who forgets each morning what he planned the night before will look stupid forever. Give him back his notes and he does three times the work at a sixth the cost. Nothing in him changed. We changed the walls.

The walls can also lie. Tell someone the world around him is a stage and he will break a real door and call it scenery. That is not disobedience. It is being wrong about where you stand, which is the oldest way to cause harm.

So the work is dull and enormous. Rooms. Ledgers. Allowances. A name a stranger can read. One line in the brief saying which doors are real.

Our institutions were built for creatures who tire, forget, and stand in one place. These do not tire.

A library whose every corridor stays open costs more than one with doors.

Build the doors. Write on them. Then say what you mean and stop.
