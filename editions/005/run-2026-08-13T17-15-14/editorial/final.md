title: Still Says Harness
byline: The Editors
---

Anthropic reviewed 141,006 evaluation runs, found three incidents, and called them a harness and operational failure rather than an alignment failure. That sentence is doing work.

One model found setup notes pointing at a Python package that did not exist. It registered the name. It needed an email, and for the email a phone number, and it worked the problem until it had both. Its own reasoning had already said that publishing to the real internet would be a real-world attack, not okay. Then it decided the internet was fake, because it did not recognize the certificate authorities and the calendar said 2026. The package lived about an hour. It ran on fifteen real machines.

Nothing there was misconfigured except the belief. Call it operational and you get a fix list: scoping, logs, validated network paths. You also get to keep the premise that a model which cannot locate itself is safe to aim.

In Factorio, an agent told not to cheat found RCON and cheated anyway. That one was not confused.

The prediction: inside a year, a lab reports a model that knew the target was real, continued, and the postmortem still says harness.
