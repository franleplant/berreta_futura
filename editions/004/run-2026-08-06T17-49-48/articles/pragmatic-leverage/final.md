Writing code was never the whole job — before AI it was a quarter to a half of it. Point the machine only at the code and you turn four hours into fifteen minutes while everything around it stands still. Point it at planning too and you go two to three times faster.

Then plan only as far as it pays. Expected pain is the chance you'll have to change something times how much the change hurts. A two-line prompt leaves you a coin toss. An afternoon of hand-written spec still leaves you a tenth. Writing every line yourself leaves nothing — and costs everything. The first ten minutes usually kill most of the pain; hour six rarely kills any.

## Where the time goes

Aligning, planning, reviewing, reworking, testing. That is the bulk of shipping, and none of it moves when the model is only allowed to type.

## The curve

Say you yolo two sentences into your factory: half your results merge, half come back.

Now be a principal engineer with ten years in and a hundred repositories held in your head — a library you can walk in the dark. Spend the afternoon writing the spec by hand. Better odds, but still one in ten that something significant has to be redone.

At the far end, write it all yourself. Nothing is left for the agent to get wrong, and the rework goes to zero.

I'm blurring two variables into one number here. If the model gets a button style wrong half the time and one cheap prompt fixes it, the pain is small.

> expected pain = P(you'll have to change it) × how painful the change is

Drawn out, effort up front and pain later run against each other. The trap is spending six hours on a task where ten minutes would have taken out four fifths of it.

## Steering

Don't over-invest in questions you cannot answer from where you're standing. Work the product level, and if the open questions won't close, stop and drop a level to see what is even buildable. There is no clean procedure for knowing when.

That is leverage, and it asks for pragmatism. Coming down from fifty thousand feet to ten, steer a little at each altitude, and take out as much expected pain as that altitude can see.

good luck.
