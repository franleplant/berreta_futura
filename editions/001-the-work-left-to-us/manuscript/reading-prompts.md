title: Four questions to carry through the source
---

## 1. Which milestone, exactly?

When a prediction depends on "AGI," "superintelligence," or "recursive self-improvement," ask what observable event the term names and which later consequences truly follow from it.

## 2. What lies outside the laboratory?

Look for the complementary work a capability still needs: reliable products, suitable data, organizational redesign, professional standards, regulation, accountability, infrastructure, and trust.

## 3. Who defines successful evaluation?

Evaluation sounds technical, but every metric contains a theory of value. Who selected the objective? Which failures count? Who bears the cost of a false positive, a false negative, or an unmeasured harm?

## 4. Does augmentation preserve agency?

A tool may increase output while weakening understanding or dependence. Ask whether it expands considered choice, develops expertise, and keeps responsibility visible - or merely makes delegation easier.
