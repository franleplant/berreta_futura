Source articles in this magazine default to faithful editing. Navigation, promotions, duplicated boilerplate, typographic noise, and extraction errors may be removed or repaired automatically. Changes to substantive wording, order, or emphasis require explicit review.

Every source edition receives a fidelity report accounting for retained text, boilerplate removals, substantive cuts, modified sentences, reordered paragraphs, and editor additions. Unlabeled additions are forbidden.

This first physical proof contains original editorial and reading apparatus rather than a pseudo-reprint. The faithful source block remains gated until its private-source handling or reproduction status is explicitly approved.
