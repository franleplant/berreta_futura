*The Work Left to Us* is Issue 001 of an independent, private reading magazine built from material discovered across the open web.

The opening editorial, cover copy, and publication design are original to this edition. The issue contains faithful private editions of Arvind Narayanan's ICML 2026 invited keynote, *What will be left for us to work on?*; Unmesh Joshi's *DSLs Enable Reliable Use of LLMs*; Satya Nadella's *The Reverse Information Paradox*; and Demis Hassabis's *A Framework for Frontier AI and the Dawning of a New Age*. Source authors and publishers retain all rights in their work.

This prototype was assembled with machine assistance for source discovery, extraction, editorial checking, production, and layout. Editorial selection, interpretation, approval, and responsibility remain human decisions.

This edition is private and not for sale. The source articles remain rights-restricted and are not cleared for public faithful republication. Additions are labeled. No source text is presented as original magazine writing, and no original commentary is presented as a source author's view.

Set for A5 trim and home booklet printing on A4 paper.
