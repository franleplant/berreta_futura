*The Work Left to Us* is Issue 001 of an independent, private reading magazine built from material discovered across the open web.

The opening editorial, source introduction, reading map, annotations, cover copy, and publication design are original to this edition. The featured source is Arvind Narayanan's ICML 2026 invited keynote, *What will be left for us to work on?* Source authors retain all rights in their work.

This prototype was assembled with machine assistance for source discovery, extraction, editorial checking, production, and layout. Editorial selection, interpretation, approval, and responsibility remain human decisions.

Faithful source material is reproduced only where private handling or explicit permission permits. Additions are labeled. No source text is presented as original magazine writing, and no original commentary is presented as the source author's view.

Set for A5 trim and home booklet printing on A4 paper.  
Issue 001 - July 2026 - Private prototype - Not for sale.
