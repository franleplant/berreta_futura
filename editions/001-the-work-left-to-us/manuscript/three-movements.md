## I. From invention to consequence

Technical capability is only the beginning. Products, organizations, regulation, adoption, and social adaptation determine what an invention eventually does in the world.

## II. Separate the milestones

Recursive self-improvement, capability discontinuity, economic transformation, autonomous power, and superintelligence are different claims. An argument that moves between them must supply the missing links.

## III. From rowing to steering

As execution becomes easier to automate, responsibility moves toward deciding, evaluating, directing, and preserving understanding. The work does not vanish; its center of gravity changes.

Together, these movements replace technological destiny with a longer and more demanding story about institutions and agency.
