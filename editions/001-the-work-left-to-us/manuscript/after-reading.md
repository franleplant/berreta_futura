title: Keep the difficult questions
---

What part of your own work is execution, and what part is judgment?

Which parts of that judgment can be explained, tested, or taught - and which depend on experience that your institution is quietly failing to reproduce?

Where would automation genuinely expand your agency? Where would it create a dependence that only looks like convenience at first?

If a capability becomes cheap tomorrow, which product decisions, organizational changes, professional standards, or political fights still stand between the demonstration and its consequences?

The next issue begins wherever an interesting link refuses to remain only a link.
