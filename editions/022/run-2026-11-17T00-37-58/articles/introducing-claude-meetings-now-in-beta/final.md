Claude can now join your video calls as a visible participant, follow the conversation and start on work during the meeting. Your team asks it questions out loud or in chat. By default it answers in writing, and it can use your connected tools with your permission. It builds docs and whiteboards with you live and writes the notes and action items, so no one on the call has to. Claude Meetings is in beta, rolling out on Team and Enterprise plans, starting with Google Meet.

## In a meeting

Say your team is planning a customer event. You say, "Claude, start a doc for this plan," and Claude writes up what the team has said so far. Teammates on the invite edit the plan while you keep talking. Someone asks what last year's event cost; Claude asks for their approval to look it up, posts it in the chat and compares it to the current plan. Someone asks for the timeline on a whiteboard, and the team works on the schedule beside the doc. Before the call ends, someone asks Claude to share a recap.

Afterwards, the notes, action items, plan and whiteboard are on the meeting's page in claude.ai. You can ask Claude about meetings you were part of, or add it to the next one from your calendar. We're working with partners like Gong, Attention, Granola, Fireflies.ai, Otter and Plaud, so you'll soon be able to send a meeting's transcript to the tools your team already uses.

## Security and privacy

Each meeting gets its own page with the notes, transcript and anything Claude built. People from your organization on the invite can see it and edit what Claude made, and it can be shared within your organization.

By default, Claude stays out of calls when someone from outside your organization is invited, and leaves if an outsider joins partway through. An admin can allow those calls. There, Claude posts its notice in the chat but doesn't reply there, and what guests say goes into the notes and transcript.

Claude uses each person's connected tools with that person's permission. It asks the first time, and a yes applies going forward. For now, when someone asks out loud, Claude asks each time to confirm who's speaking. An answer in the call's chat is visible to everyone on the call.

When Claude joins, it appears as a participant and posts a notice in the chat. It saves a transcript, summary and screen descriptions, not audio.

Before you add Claude or turn on auto-join, make sure everyone on your calls is OK with being transcribed.

## Getting started

Claude joins when someone adds it or has auto-join on. Only Google Meet is supported for now; other platforms are coming soon. On Team plans, inviting Claude is available by default, and an admin can turn it off. On Enterprise plans, an admin turns it on for all or part of the organization. During the beta it isn't available to some organizations, including those with zero data retention, HIPAA-ready configuration or customer-managed encryption keys.

During the beta, notes, transcripts and answers in your calls are free on Team and Enterprise plans, unless an answer uses a connected tool, which counts toward your plan's usage. Docs, whiteboards and other artifacts Claude makes in a call are free through February 10, 2027. Setup steps are in the Help Center.
