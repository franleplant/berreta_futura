# Issue 002 candidate: Bounds of Intelligence

Status: source intake complete; edition selection proposed; durable private captures and faithful manuscripts pending.

## Editorial premise

The shared subject is not intelligence by itself. It is the boundary around intelligence: the structures that decide what a model may express, what a firm retains as it learns, and what society permits at the frontier.

The three pieces move outward through three scales:

1. **Code** — Unmesh Joshi argues that abstractions and domain-specific languages constrain generation and supply deterministic validation.
2. **The firm** — Satya Nadella argues that organizations need a trust boundary around their traces, corrections, evaluations, memory, and learning loop.
3. **Society** — Demis Hassabis argues for an adaptive standards body that can evaluate frontier systems and escalate safeguards as capabilities change.

That progression gives the opening editorial a strong claim to test: the most important AI products may be boundaries rather than models. The editorial should also press on the incentives behind each proposal. A useful boundary must constrain its sponsor too.
